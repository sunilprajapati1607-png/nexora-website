Real screenshots • not a mockup

Inside Nexora — every screen, straight off the shop-floor PC

Nine screens from the installed desktop application running an actual saved calculation: from the dashboard that tells you what is broken, through the Bill of Materials that reconciles material and conversion cost to the rupee, to the 3D view that draws the bag and takes it apart. Click any screen to enlarge.

01 Dashboard02 Workload03 Calculation04 Structures05 RM Master06 Route Builder07 BOM08 BOM Roll-up09 3D Bag View
SCREEN 01 • DASHBOARD

It tells you what is wrong before you ask

The first screen is a to-do list, not a welcome page. Six live figures across the top, then everything the data says needs fixing, each with a button that takes you straight there.

  • Cost per bag, material share and waste across every recent calculation
  • "Needs attention" flags processes with no rate, constructions with no route, calculations that cannot yet produce a BOM
  • Out-of-tolerance bags called out against their own weight band
SCREEN 02 • WORKLOAD

Where the work actually is

Usage by construction, most-used first, with the average bag weight beside each one — then the recent calculation log with weight, deviation against target and cost per bag.

  • Spot instantly which construction dominates your order book
  • Every row shows weight, ± vs target and cost per bag together
  • A construction with no route is labelled as such, because it cannot be costed
SCREEN 03 • CALCULATION

Every gram traced back to the formula that produced it

The calculation screen splits the bag into components, then shows the full traceability chain beside it — cut length, body width and length, area, base-fabric weight, denier, UL GPM and LAMI GPM.

  • Component-wise breakdown with each part's share of total bag weight
  • Click any field to see how that value was derived
  • GSM quick-adjust stays pinned while you scroll, recalculating live
  • What-if analysis tests a change without touching the saved calculation
SCREEN 04 • STRUCTURES

29 bag constructions, each with its own field set

Every construction carries its own scope tags and active field count — a 1L stitch bag needs 27 fields, a block bottom with patch needs 80. The engine only asks for what that construction actually uses.

  • Scope tags: laminated, block bottom, open mouth, liner, backseam, easy-open, patch metallised
  • Field counts make it obvious how much complexity each construction carries
  • Duplicate an existing structure to build a variant in seconds
SCREEN 05 • RM MASTER

A new price never overwrites the old one

Materials sit in groups — granule, BOPP film, handle — and each keeps a versioned price history with effective-from dates, so a resin rate change can never silently rewrite a locked production batch.

  • Price versions with effective-from dates and notes
  • "Used in" shows every route and step that consumes the material, and at what basis
  • Default waste percentage held against the material itself
SCREEN 06 • ROUTE BUILDER

Build the route the way the plant actually runs

Drag processes into order — Tape, Weaving, Lamination, Flexo Printing, Finishing, Segregation, Packing — and each step declares what it consumes and what it produces.

  • Input → output material type shown on every process
  • A sense check warns when a step expects something no earlier step produces
  • Warnings never block a save — guidance, not a rule
  • Routes link back to the constructions that use them
SCREEN 07 • BOM

Material plus conversion, reconciled to the rupee

The BOM turns a saved calculation into a per-order material requirement — kilograms and running metres — with material cost and conversion cost rolled up separately and then proved against each other.

  • Material, conversion, total and cost per bag as four live figures
  • Each material line shows kg for basis, rate, cost and share of total
  • A green reconciliation line confirms the BOM total matches the roll-up exactly
SCREEN 08 • BOM ROLL-UP

Waste carried forward, never credited back

Conversion lines show the kilograms passing through each process at its own rate. Kilograms carry the waste of every stage they pass through — scrap is still paid for, so "lost to waste" is money gone, not a figure deducted from the total.

  • Process-level conversion cost with % of total
  • Every figure is read from the roll-up above, never recomputed
  • Saved calculations and their BOMs listed together, with cost per bag computed live
SCREEN 09 • 3D BAG VIEW

The bag drawn, turned and taken apart

The newest screen. Nexora draws the construction your specification describes, prints the item name on its face, and lets you pull it apart part by part — each one carrying its own size, weight and share of the bag.

  • A pasted bag is drawn as a block whose ends are its patches; an open-mouth sack as the lay-flat tube
  • Body, patches, valve, handle, liner, yarn, easy-open tape and back seam, each in its own colour
  • The flat view beside it is to scale and dimensioned, so a cutting size can be checked before the loom runs
  • A parts table with size, weight and share, and the bag's total against its target
