Woven sack GSM, bag weight & RM forecast calculator

Two quick tools from the Nexora engine. The first turns denier, mesh and size into fabric GSM, bag weight and cost per bag. The second forecasts raw material and cost for a whole order. Type and the numbers update instantly.

Tool 1

Fabric GSM & bag weight

Plain single-layer body fabric on a tubular loom. The full desktop app adds patches, valves, liners, BOPP, ink and 29 constructions with saved history.

Bag specification

Assumes tubular fabric (two layers), 5% cutting and stitching allowance and 1.2 g of stitching thread. Indicative only — your plant's constants may differ.
