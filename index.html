Forecast before you quote

Weight, raw material and resources — forecast to the rupee

A wrong bag weight costs you twice: once in the quote and again in the resin you buy. Nexora forecasts the whole chain from denier and mesh to kilograms of PP, masterbatch, ink and film, then adds the resources each process consumes.

  • Bag weight — component-wise: body, patch, valve, liner, coating, BOPP, ink, adhesive, thread.
  • Raw material forecast — kg per order for every material, with waste carried through each process stage.
  • Resources — manpower, power and machine rates held per process as cost per kilogram.
  • Cost per bag — material plus conversion, reconciled, with a versioned price history behind every rate.
  • See it in 3D — the bag drawn from its own specification, pulled apart part by part, every part weighed. Watch it.

Why plants switch

From spreadsheets to one trusted number

The maths does not change. How confidently you can trust the number, prove it and repeat it does.

The spreadsheet way

  • One overwritten cell changes a costing nobody notices until dispatch
  • GSM, denier and mesh formulas re-typed differently by every operator
  • No history — you cannot prove what last month's quote was based on
  • A resin rate update silently corrupts approved batches
  • Stock, payroll and maintenance live in five different files

The Nexora way

  • One locked formula engine — the same result on every machine, every time
  • Component-level traceability from denier to finished bag weight
  • Every calculation is dated, revisioned and reproducible
  • Price revisions never overwrite locked production batches
  • Masters shared across costing, inventory, production and payroll
  • Each person signs in by name, masters shared company-wide, calculations private unless shared
