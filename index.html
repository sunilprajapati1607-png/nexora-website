The problem

The gram you miss
is the margin you lose.

Most woven sack units still price bags off a shared Excel sheet: one tab per customer, formulas nobody remembers writing, and a GSM figure nobody can defend when a buyer disputes the weight on delivery.

The spreadsheet way

  • GSM typed in by hand from a paper lookup chart, months out of date
  • No record of which denier or EPI/PPI a past quote actually used
  • Every yarn price change means re-typing every customer's rate row
  • Machine operator, supervisor and accounts each keep their own copy
  • A buyer's weight dispute means digging through old chat messages

The Nexora way

  • GSM derived live from the actual denier, EPI and PPI you enter
  • Every saved job stores the exact inputs and formula behind it
  • Change the PP rate once — every open job recalculates instantly
  • One shared record: operator enters, supervisor approves, admin sees all
  • Pull up the exact calculation behind any order, any time
Inside the engine

Built on how a
woven sack is actually made.

01

Component breakdown

Fabric, stitching thread, lamination film and printing ink costed as separate lines — not one number you have to take on faith.

02

Formula traceability

Every stored job keeps the denier, EPI/PPI, wastage % and rate it was calculated with, so a question six months later still has an answer.

03

Denier & GPM engine

GSM derived directly from warp/weft density and tape denier, replacing the printed lookup chart taped above the calculator.

04

Offline-first

Runs on the loom-house PC with patchy Wi-Fi. Data is held locally and syncs to the cloud the moment a connection returns.

05

Role-based access

Operators enter specs, supervisors approve costings, admins manage users and rate cards — enforced on the server, not just hidden in the UI.

06

Version history

Reprice instantly when raw material rates move, while the calculation behind an order already confirmed stays exactly as it was.

How it works

From spec to job card
in five steps.

Input

Enter the bag spec

Length, width, denier, EPI/PPI and lamination — the numbers already on your customer's order.

Process

Engine calculates

GSM, fabric consumption and component weights derived in real time as specs are entered.

Decision

Check against tolerance

Compare the calculated weight to the customer's agreed spec before the job goes to the floor.

Output

Weight & cost

Bag weight, material cost per bag, and cost per 1,000 — ready to quote or approve.

Action

Save & sync

Job card saved locally, synced to the cloud, and retrievable from any Nexora install.
