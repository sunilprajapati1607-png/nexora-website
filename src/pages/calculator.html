How woven bag weight is calculated

A woven sack is a tube of PP or HDPE tape fabric, cut to length, folded or pasted at the ends, and optionally coated, laminated with BOPP film, lined with an LDPE liner and printed. The weight of the bag is the sum of every layer's area multiplied by its GSM.

Fabric GSM = Denier × (warp mesh + weft mesh) ÷ 228.6
Film GSM = micron × density (BOPP ≈ 0.91, LDPE ≈ 0.92)
Tube width = 2 × (bag width + gusset)  ·  Cut length = bag length + fold allowance (stitched) or + bottom width (block bottom)
Body weight (g) = tube width × cut length ÷ 10,000 × (fabric GSM + coating GSM + film GSM)
Bag weight = body + patches + valve + liner + ink + thread

Why the plant number is different from a quick formula

Every plant runs its own fold allowance, patch depth, valve size, ink coverage and waste norm, and each bag construction uses a different set of fields. This page uses common defaults so a buyer or a sales engineer can sanity-check a quotation in a minute. Bag costing and packaging costing

Cost per bag = raw material kilograms (including process waste) × rate for each material, plus conversion cost per kilogram for extrusion, weaving, lamination, printing and finishing. The rates on this page are examples for demonstration. In production costing, resin and film prices change every week, so a rate must be dated and versioned — an approved quote keeps the rate it was costed at. That is exactly what the RM master in Nexora does, and why the component-wise breakdown here is reserved for the demo.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the GSM of woven fabric from denier?

GSM = denier × (warp mesh + weft mesh) ÷ 228.6. For 900 denier tape at 10 × 10 mesh: 900 × 20 ÷ 228.6 = 78.7 GSM. Use the denier to GSM calculator for any combination.

How much does BOPP lamination add to bag weight?

BOPP film weight in GSM is roughly micron × 0.91. An 18 micron film adds about 16.4 GSM, plus the extrusion-coated sandwich layer, typically 15–22 GSM. See the BOPP bag calculation page.

What is a block bottom bag and how is its weight different?

A block bottom (pasted or pinch bottom) bag has a rectangular bottom and top formed from the tube, with pasted patches and optionally a valve. Its cut length includes the bottom width and its patches add weight. See block bottom bag calculation.

Are the prices in the costing real?

No — they are example rates so you can see how material and conversion cost build up. Enter your own rates in the "Edit example RM prices" panel, or request a demo to cost with your own dated price master and process rates.
