Staff tracking module

Who ran what, where, and how well

Operator-wise, machine-wise, shift-wise. Output, waste and downtime per person, so productivity and piece-rate wages are computed from the same entries — and the best operators are visible, not just the loudest.

Machine allocation

Assign operators and helpers to machines per shift. Swaps and absences recorded as they happen.

Output per person

Kg, rolls, metres or bags produced by each operator, from the same production entry the supervisor already makes.

Waste per person

Waste by type and operator — the difference between a 2% and a 5% loom operator is real money each month.

Downtime & idle time

Reason-coded stoppages against the operator on duty, reconciled with maintenance breakdowns.

Skills & training

Which constructions and machines each person is cleared for; training records for audits.

Incentives

Shift and monthly incentive schemes computed from output and quality, feeding straight into payroll.

Simple capture, serious reports

One entry per shift, many answers

Supervisors enter output and waste once — on a PC or a phone at the machine. Nexora turns that into operator productivity, machine efficiency, waste trend and wage inputs without any extra typing.

  • Operator leaderboard by output and waste %, per process
  • Machine efficiency by shift and by operator on it
  • Attendance vs allocation: who was present but not on a machine
  • Optional GPS/site check-in for multi-unit or field staff

Fits with

  • HR & Payroll — attendance and piece-rate wages
  • Maintenance — downtime reasons and fitter jobs
  • Inventory — roll-wise output with loom and operator
  • Nexora ERP — production entries and reports
