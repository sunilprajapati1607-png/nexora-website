HR & payroll built for the factory floor

Loom operators on piece-rate, helpers on daily wages, staff on monthly salary, contractor labour on the side — one payroll that handles all of them, with attendance from the machine and wages that reconcile with production.

Biometric or manual punch, three-shift rosters, late marks, overtime, week-offs and holidays per plant.

Piece-rate wages

Daily & monthly pay

Daily-rated helpers, monthly staff and contractor gangs on one register with advance, loan and deduction tracking.

Payslips & registers

PF / ESI ready

Labour cost per kg

Wages flow back into the costing as the real manpower resource rate per process — forecast meets actual.

Why a factory payroll is different

Generic HR software does not know what a loom shift is

Woven sack plants pay people in three or four different ways at once, and the wage depends on production data that lives in another system. Nexora's payroll reads the production entries, so a piece-rate operator's wage for the month is computed from the rolls the loom actually produced.

  • Multiple pay structures in one plant, one register
  • Contractor labour with contractor-wise bills
  • Advances and canteen deductions recovered automatically
  • Works together with staff & shop-floor tracking
