New • ink assumption (beta)

What the artwork costs in ink, before the job runs

Ink is the one material a bag costing usually guesses. Drop in the PNG or JPG you print and Nexora reads how much of the sheet each of cyan, magenta, yellow and black covers, then returns the ink and solvent the job should draw.

Four figures, not a guess

Ink and solvent per square metre, per bag, per 1,000 bags and per kilogram of fabric or film. Add them to the BOM section as ordinary lines in kilograms per 1,000 bags and they cost like any other material.

White, three ways

No white, a patch behind the artwork only, or a flood white over the whole face. On a half-covered design the flood costs exactly twice the patch, and the line says which it is. GSM and micron carry the substrate effect.

It learns your press

Until it is trained, the figures come from published trade practice and the window says so. Record what a job actually drew and train: ten jobs make it worth trusting, a hundred make it good, and it reports WEAK, FAIR or GOOD so you know which you have.

Your artwork never leaves the plant

The image stays on your computer. What crosses the wire is four coverage fractions, a white share, a substrate and a size — a few hundred bytes, from which no image can be rebuilt. One company can never read or overwrite another's model. Each coefficient reads as a sentence a print manager can check: we lay 3.1 grams of cyan per square metre at full coverage.

Raw material & price master

A new price never overwrites the old one

Materials sit in groups — granule, masterbatch, BOPP film, ink, adhesive, handle, liner — and each keeps a versioned price history with effective-from dates. A resin rate change can never silently rewrite a locked production batch.

  • Price versions with effective-from dates and notes
  • Excel-like bulk price update when the market moves
  • "Used in" shows every route and step that consumes the material
  • Default waste percentage held against the material itself
  • Fetch prices online from a producer's published list, once a day on its own, and the BOM records where each price came from
  • A material's price can follow another material, so a grade that always runs at a fixed difference stays in step
Process, route & resources

Build the route the way the plant actually runs

Drag processes into order — tape extrusion, weaving, lamination, flexo printing, cutting, stitching, finishing, segregation, packing — and each step declares what it consumes and produces. Resource rates (manpower, electricity, machine) are held per process as cost per kilogram.

  • A bag that skips lamination is simply a shorter route, not a code change
  • A sense check warns when a step expects something no earlier step produces
  • Component-wise BOM: body, patch and liner can each run their own route
  • Saved workflows: set the recipe up once, load it on the next bag of that construction
  • Every money figure also shown per kilogram, so a rate can be sanity-checked at a glance
Costed BOM & roll-up

Material plus conversion, reconciled to the rupee

The BOM turns a saved calculation into a per-order material requirement — kilograms and running metres — with material cost and conversion cost rolled up separately and then proved against each other. Waste is carried forward, never credited back: scrap is still paid for.

  • Material, conversion, total and cost per bag as four live figures
  • Process-level conversion cost with % of total
  • A green reconciliation line confirms the BOM matches the roll-up exactly
  • Print, PDF and Excel export of the calculation sheet and BOM

Frequently asked

Does it work offline on the shop-floor PC?

Yes. Nexora is an offline-first Windows desktop application. Data lives on the machine, with backup and restore. The licensed edition checks in with the licence service and can be granted an offline allowance per company.

Can I edit the constants and formulas?

The Constants Master is yours to edit, with per-construction overrides, and the Formula Traceability card shows every formula on screen by design. The engine itself is locked so every machine gives the same answer.

How does it handle a resin price change?

A new price is a new version with its own effective-from date. Approved calculations keep the rate they were costed at; new ones pick up the new rate. Bulk update is Excel-like when the whole market moves.

Which bag constructions are covered?

29 standard structures: plain and printed PP/HDPE woven sacks, BOPP laminated, block bottom, pinch bottom, valve, liner, handle, backseam and easy-open variants, each with its own field set and weight logic. New constructions can be added as structures, not code.

Can more than one person use it in the same company?

Yes. Each person signs in with their own name and PIN on any licensed seat. Materials, prices, processes and routes are shared company-wide and the latest save wins by the server's clock, while calculations stay private unless your administrator grants wider access.

What is the 3D bag view for?

It draws the bag your specification describes so a wrong patch depth or valve size is visible at a glance, and it breaks the bag into parts with a size, weight and share against each. See the recording above.

How does the ink assumption work, and is my artwork safe?

Nexora reads the artwork on your own computer and works out the coverage of each colour. Only those coverage figures, the white share, the substrate and the size are sent for the calculation — never the image, and never to another company's model. Untrained it uses published trade practice; trained on your measured jobs it uses your own press.

Is this the Nexora ERP?

It is the technical calculation module that the wider Nexora ERP is built around. Masters created here — materials, prices, processes, routes — carry into the ERP modules.
