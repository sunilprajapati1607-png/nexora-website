Inventory module

Inventory & fabric roll traceability

Know exactly how much granule is in the godown, which loom made which roll, where that roll went and how many bags came out of it. Barcode-scanned, roll-wise, and reconciled with the RM forecast of every order.

Raw material stock

PP/HDPE granule, masterbatch, filler, LD, BOPP film, ink, adhesive, thread — batch-wise with supplier, GRN and rate.

Roll-wise fabric

Every roll gets a barcode at the loom: width, GSM, length, weight, loom number, operator and shift.

WIP through the route

Rolls move through lamination, printing and cutting with their history intact — no lost rolls, no "which roll was this?".

Finished goods & bales

Bale-wise finished stock against sales orders, packing lists and dispatch — with pending-order visibility.

Forecast vs issue

RM issued to an order compared with the forecast kg from the costed BOM, so variance is visible while it can still be fixed.

Scan on phone or PC

Barcode scanning from an Android phone or a wired scanner — proven in a live woven fabric roll traceability app.

Reports

Stock answers a plant manager actually asks

  • Stock on hand by material, batch and location, valued at the price master
  • Roll ageing: fabric lying unconverted for more than N days
  • Loom-wise and shift-wise fabric output and waste
  • Material consumption per order vs forecast, with the rupee variance
  • Reorder alerts against planned orders, not just minimum levels

Proven on a real floor
