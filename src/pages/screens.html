✦ NEXORA AI • ONE SENTENCE

Say the bag and ask for the cost — Nexora AI shows the steps

“1L stitch bag, 490 × 550, single fold, 70 gram, 40×40, tape 80+20, give me the cost” — spoken or typed, in ગુજરાતી, हिन्दी or English, on any window.

  • Calculation, route or saved workflow, which part goes where, every stage’s recipe and waste, a check, the cost
  • “70 gram” is the target weight — the GSM is found by Nexora; a weight in grams always wins over a GSM
  • Nothing is done until you press Run; say “make it 75 gram” and the same bag changes
✦ NEXORA AI • RUN

One Run — weighed, routed, checked, costed

Every step is ticked or says why it stopped. The cost is the BOM’s own figure, worked out on your computer — Nexora AI never sees it, nor an item name or a customer.

  • It checks what it built before the cost: “is everything right?”
  • It learns from ALL your saved bags and BOMs — and from every change you make to what it did
  • It thinks like your technical manager: asks every open field of the structure, puts in the stages the bag needs (a 2L bag gets lamination) — and drafts the quotation
  • One conversation on every window; change the plan where it stands, move it, size it, make it small
✦ NEXORA AI • FILL THIS FORM

Or say the bag — or show a photo — and it fills the form

Speak or type the specification in ગુજરાતી, हिन्दी or English, or show a photo, a drawing or a PDF. What was heard is shown; what is missing is asked.

  • Construction, size, GSM, mesh, BOPP and bag quantity, placed on your own constructions and fields
  • A dropdown where a field has options, a box where it is a number
  • The engine weighs it as ever — nothing is saved until you save
✦ NEXORA AI • BOM CHECK

It reads a BOM and says what to fix

Stages, sources, recipes and waste, read in plain words — with a link to the stage. It never sees the cost of a bag.

  • A stage taking the wrong material, a recipe that does not add up, a waste that looks wrong
  • In English, ગુજરાતી or हिन्दी — it answers in the language you use
  • It changes nothing: every figure is still the engine’s
FEATURE 01 • DASHBOARD

It tells you what is wrong before you ask

The first screen is a to-do list, not a welcome page: live figures across the top, then everything the data says needs fixing, each with a button that takes you straight there.

  • Cost per bag, material share and out-of-tolerance bags across every recent calculation
  • "Needs attention" flags materials with no price, processes with no rate, constructions with no route
  • Licence usage, the last fourteen days, and what the masters still need
FEATURE 02 • WEIGHT CALCULATION

Every gram traced back to the formula that produced it

Pick the construction, type the size and the GSM, and the bag is weighed as you type — body, patches, valve, handle, liner, yarn — with the full traceability chain beside it: cut length, area, denier, UL and LAMI GPM.

  • Component-wise breakdown with each part's share of the bag
  • Target weight, tolerance band and variance, live
  • The Documents line links the calculation to its BOM and its quotations — one number system, like an ERP
FEATURE 03 • 3D BAG VIEW

The bag drawn, turned and taken apart

Nexora draws the construction your specification describes, prints the item name on its face, and lets you pull it apart part by part — each one carrying its own size, weight and share.

  • Body, patches, valve, handle, liner, yarn, easy-open tape and back seam, each in its own colour
  • A dimensioned lay-flat view beside it, to scale, so a cutting size is checked before the loom runs
  • Turn, disassemble, print
FEATURE 04 • RM MASTER & PRICE TRACKING

A new price never overwrites the old one

Materials sit in groups — granule, masterbatch, BOPP film, ink, handle — and each keeps a versioned price history with effective-from dates, so a resin rate change can never silently rewrite a costed batch.

  • Price versions with effective-from dates and notes; the rate on any date is the newest not in the future
  • Fetch a published polymer price list online, or paste one in like a spreadsheet
  • "Used in" shows every route and step that consumes the material
FEATURE 05 • COSTED BILL OF MATERIALS

Material, conversion, waste — reconciled to the rupee

Pick a saved calculation and the BOM costs it stage by stage along its route: kilograms and running metres per material, waste at every step, conversion cost from the resource rates, and the cost per bag the quotation will stand on.

  • The costed list's total always equals the roll-up's total — if it ever did not, the card would say so in red
  • Save the set-up as a workflow and the next bag of that type loads it in one click
  • Every saved BOM has its own number; the register below lists them all with their cost per bag
FEATURE 06 • QUOTATION

A price for a buyer — one row per bag, nothing of your cost on it

Pick the saved calculations, quote per bag, per thousand or per kilogram, add freight and packing, choose GST within or outside the state, tick the terms — and Nexora does the arithmetic. Your cost per bag is on screen while you quote and never on the sheet.

  • Numbered on save under your own document series (QT-2026-000001, or whatever your plant uses)
  • Linked to the calculation and the BOM it stands on — the Documents line shows all three
  • Terms and conditions written once, ticked per quotation
FEATURE 07 • INK ASSUMPTION (BETA)

It reads your artwork and says what a job will draw

Give it the artwork you print. The studio measures how much of the sheet each ink covers and estimates what the job should draw — cyan, magenta, yellow, black and the white patch — for the substrate, the faces printed and the bag size.

  • Trained on what jobs actually drew: tell it the real figure and the model tightens
  • Feeds the printing section of the BOM as an assumption, marked as one
FEATURE 08 • EASY COST

A price in ten seconds, for the buyer who is on the phone

Six choices and a rupee figure, on the trade’s own gram. It is a demo for a quick look with no accuracy guaranteed — the real cost comes from the construction, the route, the recipes and your own dated prices — and the window says so on its face.

  • Seventy-one rates, all editable, all arriving as round samples rather than a price list
  • Linked to nothing: no route, no BOM, no saved calculation, and nothing typed in it is kept
