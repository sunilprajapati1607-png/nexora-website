FEATURE 01 • DASHBOARD

It tells you what is wrong before you ask

The first screen is a to-do list, not a welcome page: live figures across the top, then everything the data says needs fixing, each with a button that takes you straight there.

  • Cost per bag, material share and out-of-tolerance bags across every recent calculation
  • "Needs attention" flags materials with no price, processes with no rate, constructions with no route
  • Licence usage, the last fourteen days, and what the masters still need
FEATURE 02 • WEIGHT CALCULATION

Every gram traced back to the formula that produced it

Pick the construction, type the size and the GSM, and the bag is weighed as you type — body, patches, valve, handle, liner, yarn — with the full traceability chain beside it: cut length, area, denier, UL and LAMI GPM.

  • Component-wise breakdown with each part's share of the bag
  • Target weight, tolerance band and variance, live
  • The Documents line links the calculation to its BOM and its quotations — one number system, like an ERP
FEATURE 03 • 3D BAG VIEW

The bag drawn, turned and taken apart

Nexora draws the construction your specification describes, prints the item name on its face, and lets you pull it apart part by part — each one carrying its own size, weight and share.

  • Body, patches, valve, handle, liner, yarn, easy-open tape and back seam, each in its own colour
  • A dimensioned lay-flat view beside it, to scale, so a cutting size is checked before the loom runs
  • Turn, disassemble, print
FEATURE 04 • CALCULATION SHEET

Print preview, exactly as it prints

The calculation sheet — specification, result, component breakdown and the formula trace — previewed on screen before it goes to the printer or to a PDF. White paper whatever theme the screen is in.

  • Print, Print direct, Export to PDF and Export to Excel from the same bar
  • Your company name and logo on the sheet
  • Sent by email or WhatsApp in one press
FEATURE 05 • STRUCTURE MASTER

Twenty-nine real constructions, delivered — and yours added beside them

Every bag type the plant makes is a structure: which technical fields it uses, which it does not. Delivered structures are never lost — their fields are a floor — and a retired one is set inactive rather than deleted.

  • Stitched, block bottom, easy open, BOPP, liner, handle, patch — with the fields each one needs
  • Add and edit freely; duplicate a delivered one to make a variant
  • Golden arrows open the construction from anywhere it is named
FEATURE 06 • RM MASTER & PRICE TRACKING

A new price never overwrites the old one

Materials sit in groups — granule, masterbatch, BOPP film, ink, handle — and each keeps a versioned price history with effective-from dates, so a resin rate change can never silently rewrite a costed batch.

  • Price versions with effective-from dates and notes; the rate on any date is the newest not in the future
  • Fetch a published polymer price list online, or paste one in like a spreadsheet
  • "Used in" shows every route and step that consumes the material
FEATURE 07 • PROCESS & ROUTE

The bag's path through the plant, and the BOM follows it

A route is an ordered list of processes — tape, weaving, lamination, printing, cutting, stitching, packing — each with its recipe and its resource rates. Link it to a construction and the BOM builds its sections from that route.

  • Processes with manpower, power and machine rates per kilogram or per bag
  • A route checks itself: what each step consumes must be what an earlier step produced
  • A bag that skips lamination simply has a shorter route — the calculation does not change
FEATURE 08 • COSTED BILL OF MATERIALS

Material, conversion, waste — reconciled to the rupee

Pick a saved calculation and the BOM costs it stage by stage along its route: kilograms and running metres per material, waste at every step, conversion cost from the resource rates, and the cost per bag the quotation will stand on.

  • The costed list's total always equals the roll-up's total — if it ever did not, the card would say so in red
  • Save the set-up as a workflow and the next bag of that type loads it in one click
  • Every saved BOM has its own number; the register below lists them all with their cost per bag
FEATURE 09 • BOM SHEET

The BOM and cost roll-up, previewed before it prints

Stage by stage, material by material, with the conversion cost and the totals — the sheet a costing desk pins up. Print it, export it to PDF or Excel, or send it on.

  • Order quantity, finished kilograms and cost per bag on one page
  • Excel export with the same figures, for the accounts desk
FEATURE 10 • QUOTATION

A price for a buyer — one row per bag, nothing of your cost on it

Pick the saved calculations, quote per bag, per thousand or per kilogram, add freight and packing, choose GST within or outside the state, tick the terms — and Nexora does the arithmetic. Your cost per bag is on screen while you quote and never on the sheet.

  • Numbered on save under your own document series (QT-2026-000001, or whatever your plant uses)
  • Linked to the calculation and the BOM it stands on — the Documents line shows all three
  • Terms and conditions written once, ticked per quotation
FEATURE 11 • QUOTATION SHEET

The quotation as the buyer will hold it

Your letterhead, the buyer, each bag with its specification, quantity and rate, the charges, the tax, the total and the terms — previewed, printed or saved as a PDF named after its number.

  • No recipe, no material price, no margin, no formula — ever
  • Validity, payment and delivery terms carried from the last quotation
FEATURE 12 • INK ASSUMPTION (BETA)

It reads your artwork and says what a job will draw

Give it the artwork you print. The studio measures how much of the sheet each ink covers and estimates what the job should draw — cyan, magenta, yellow, black and the white patch — for the substrate, the faces printed and the bag size.

  • Trained on what jobs actually drew: tell it the real figure and the model tightens
  • Feeds the printing section of the BOM as an assumption, marked as one
FEATURE 13 • NOTES

Your own pad, with @ that knows your documents

A costing desk keeps a pad beside the keyboard. Nexora's is stamped with your name and the time, stays on your computer and is never shared — and typing @ lists your calculations, BOMs, quotations, materials, structures, routes, processes and the people on the company, newest first, so a note can point at the record it is about.

  • A number or a name on a note is a chip that opens the record
  • Personal to you; the company conversation is next door
FEATURE 14 • COMPANY CHAT

One room for the plant — and only the plant

Everybody signed in to your company licence shares one conversation, on every window. A calculation number, a BOM number, a quotation, a material, a construction or a colleague's name becomes a chip that opens the record — and what travels is the number, never the costing.

  • Tag a colleague with @ — a message that mentions you says so
  • A new message raises a small popup beside the button; the button counts what is unread
  • Nothing here leaves the plant; nothing can be quietly rewritten
FEATURE 15 • STRUCTURE EDITOR

Tick the fields a construction uses — the delivered ones stay

Every technical field, grouped as the calculation groups them: dimensions, fabric and lamination, BOPP, metallised, liner, handle, easy open, advanced. A field ticked on appears in the calculation window for that construction; a delivered field is ticked and greyed, because it always shows.

  • A field can be added to a delivered construction, never taken away — duplicate it for a thinner variant
  • Save, or save and start the next one
FEATURE 16 • NEXORA CONTACT

Feedback and problem reports, from inside the application

Help → Nexora Contact sends feedback, or a problem report that takes a picture of the screen before its own dialog opens. Both reach Nexora with the plant, the window and the version filled in; a computer that is offline keeps the report and sends it later.

  • Every report lands in Nexora's licence console and on the owner's phone within minutes
  • The numbers are in the dialog too: +91 92134 15996 • +91 75671 61607
