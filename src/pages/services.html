Service 01

ERP / SAP implementation & support

Our founder has rolled out SAP on real woven sack shop floors and runs it today as SAP Manager. If you are implementing an ERP — Nexora or otherwise — you get someone who knows both the system and the plant.

  • Process mapping and gap analysis against how the plant really runs
  • Master data design: items, BOMs, routes, work centres, prices
  • Configuration, testing, user training and go-live hand-holding
  • Post-go-live support, month-end closing and report building
  • SAP data observation tools and gap-analysis dashboards

Why it goes better with an insider

Most ERP failures in this industry are master data failures: a bag modelled as one item instead of components, routes hard-coded, waste ignored. We have made and fixed those mistakes already.
