Forecast before you quote

Weight, raw material and resources — forecast to the rupee

A wrong bag weight costs you twice: once in the quote and again in the resin you buy. Nexora forecasts the whole chain from denier and mesh to kilograms of PP, masterbatch, ink and film, then adds the resources each process consumes.

  • Bag weight — component-wise: body, patch, valve, liner, coating, BOPP, ink, adhesive, thread.
  • Raw material forecast — kg per order for every material, with waste carried through each process stage.
  • Resources — manpower, power and machine rates held per process as cost per kilogram.
  • Cost per bag — material plus conversion, reconciled, with a versioned price history behind every rate.
