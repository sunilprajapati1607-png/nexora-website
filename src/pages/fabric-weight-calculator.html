Width × length × GSM = weight calculator

The most used formula in a packaging plant: area times GSM gives grams. Use it for a single bag panel, a tubular bag (two layers), a cut piece of laminated fabric, or a whole roll.

The formula

Weight (g) = width (cm) × length (cm) ÷ 10,000 × layers × GSM
Inches: multiply width and length by 2.54 first
Roll (kg) = width (m) × length (m) × GSM ÷ 1000

Example: a 50 × 90 cm tubular bag at 80 GSM. Area = 0.50 × 0.90 = 0.45 m² per layer, two layers = 0.90 m², weight = 0.90 × 80 = 72 g of fabric. Add fold allowance, coating, liner, ink and thread for the finished bag — the bag weight & costing calculator does all of that per structure.

FAQ

Should I use cut length or finished length?

Cut length. A stitched sack needs a top hem and bottom fold, so cut length is finished length plus 3–5 cm; a block bottom bag adds the bottom width.

How do I include lamination?

Use the laminated GSM: fabric GSM + coating GSM (+ BOPP film GSM). The BOPP bag calculator builds it from micron.
