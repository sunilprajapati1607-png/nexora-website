The person behind the engine

Sunil's career did not start in software. Four years as a department head, then four years in production planning — where ISO documentation, customer audits and third-party audits made master data discipline non-negotiable. From there he moved into SAP implementation, rolling out ERP on real shop floors, and today leads as SAP Manager. Every one of those roles is stitched into how Nexora is built.

The tools grew alongside the roles — Excel on the production floor, AppSheet to close the gaps ERP could not reach fast enough, then custom software once the problem outgrew both. It is why Nexora carries an SAP touch: structured master data, revision history and audit-ready documents are not an afterthought here. They are how Sunil has run production for sixteen years.

How we work

Principles that shape every product

Never overwrite

A new price, a new approval, a new revision — always a new version. History is how you prove a quote and pass an audit.

Masters first

Items, constructions, materials, processes and routes are defined once and shared. Everything else is a transaction against them.

Works where the plant is

Offline-first on the shop-floor PC. Your calculations, materials and prices stay on your machine.

Show the formula

Every derived value can be traced to the formula and inputs that produced it. No black boxes on a costing.

Data, not customisation

A new construction or route is a master entry, not a change request. The plant should be able to grow without waiting for us.

One call away

You talk to the person who built it. Support on WhatsApp, updates included in the AMC.
