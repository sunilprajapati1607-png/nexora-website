How block bottom bag weight is calculated

A block bottom bag (also called a pasted bottom, pinch bottom or AD*STAR-type bag) is made from a gusseted or flat woven tube whose ends are folded into a rectangular bottom and top and closed with pasted patches. A valve bag adds a valve strip at one corner for filling. Compared with a stitched sack, three things change the weight:

  • Cut length includes the bottom width: cut length = bag length + bottom width.
  • Patches: two laminated fabric pieces (top and bottom), each roughly bag width × patch depth plus a margin.
  • Valve strip: a short laminated strip, typically bottom width × 12–15 cm.
Tube width = 2 × (bag width + bottom width)
Body weight = tube width × (length + bottom width) ÷ 10,000 × (fabric GSM + coating GSM + BOPP GSM)
Patches = 2 × (width + 2 cm) × (patch depth + 2 cm) ÷ 10,000 × (fabric GSM + coating GSM)
Valve = (bottom width + 2 cm) × 14 cm ÷ 10,000 × (fabric GSM + coating GSM)
Bag weight = body + patches + valve + ink (no stitching thread)

Typical block bottom specifications

  • Cement 50 kg valve bag: 1000–1100 denier, 12 × 12 mesh, 20–25 GSM coating, 48–52 cm wide, 60–65 cm long, 10 cm bottom, no BOPP.
  • BOPP block bottom 10–25 kg (rice, pet food, seeds): 800–900 denier, 10 × 10 to 12 × 12 mesh, 18–20 GSM coating, 18–25 µm BOPP.
  • Putty / tile adhesive 20–40 kg: 1000 denier, 12 × 12 mesh, valve type, sometimes with a PE liner.

Plants differ in patch overlap, valve length and bottom forming allowance, so treat the figures here as a first check. The Nexora desktop app models block bottom constructions with 80 fields including patch, valve, metallised film and liner, each traceable to its formula.

FAQ

What is the difference between block bottom and pinch bottom?

Both form a rectangular bottom from the tube. A block bottom (pasted) bag closes the bottom with a pasted patch; a pinch bottom folds and hot-melt seals the end. Weight calculation is nearly the same; pinch bottom bags have a smaller patch or none.

How much does a patch and valve add?

For a 50 × 60 cm cement bag with 10 cm bottom at 86 GSM laminated fabric, the two patches add about 10–11 g and the valve strip about 1.5 g on a bag of roughly 75 g.

Can I calculate a BOPP block bottom bag?

Yes — choose "BOPP block bottom bag" in the structure list. The BOPP micron field appears and the film weight is added to the body.
