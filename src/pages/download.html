Read this before you download

  • Download only from this page. Every official Nexora download link is on nexoraofficial.org. Do not install a Nexora setup file sent by anyone else, on WhatsApp, e-mail or a pen drive, because a changed file can harm your computer and your data.
  • Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. The setup file is not yet signed with a paid Microsoft certificate, so SmartScreen shows this warning for a new file. If the file name is exactly Nexora-Setup-{{suiteVersion}}.exe and it came from this page, click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Your antivirus may scan or hold the file. Let the scan finish. If your antivirus blocks it, contact us before you change any antivirus setting.
  • You need a Nexora company code to sign in. The software opens to the sign-in screen. Your company code, user name and PIN come from Nexora, or from your company admin. to get a demo company.
  • Internet is needed. Sign-in, sync between computers and phones, Nexora AI and updates all work through the Nexora service.
  • Results are indicative. Check every weight, BOM and cost against your own plant constants and samples before you quote. See the terms of use & disclaimer.
  • Do not share the setup or your sign-in. A licence seat belongs to one person. Ask your company admin to add a new user instead.
