Maintenance module

Maintenance & machine care for the whole line

Tape lines, circular looms, lamination, flexo printers, cutting and stitching — log every breakdown, schedule preventive work by running hours, track spares, and see which machine is quietly costing you the most.

Breakdown logging

Operator raises it from the floor; fitter closes it with cause, action, spares used and minutes lost. Nothing on paper.

Preventive schedules

Checklists by machine type, triggered by calendar or running hours read from production entries.

Spares & consumables

Spares stock, minimum levels, issue against a job, and machine-wise spares cost history.

Machine master

Every loom, extruder and printer with make, model, installation date, AMC, warranty and documents.

Downtime analysis

Downtime by machine, cause and shift; MTBF and MTTR; repeat failures flagged before they become habits.

Cost of downtime

Lost minutes × machine rate from the costing masters = what the breakdown actually cost, in rupees.

Connected to production

Downtime you can see in the same reports as output

Because the maintenance module reads the same machine master and production entries as the rest of Nexora, a loom's downtime shows up next to its output, its waste and its operator — not in a separate register nobody reconciles.

  • Running-hour based preventive triggers, no manual meter reading
  • Mobile-friendly for the fitter on the floor
  • Works with staff tracking for fitter productivity
  • Available standalone or as a Nexora ERP module
