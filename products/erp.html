Forecast vs actual

Close the loop between the quote and the plant

The cost you quoted was a forecast. Production entries, RM issues and waste bring in the actuals. Nexora ERP puts them side by side per order, so you learn which construction, machine or shift eats the margin.

  • Forecast RM kg vs issued kg per order and per material
  • Planned bag weight vs QC-sampled weight, against the tolerance band
  • Conversion cost forecast vs actual machine hours, power and manpower
  • Waste by process, with the money it took with it
Why Nexora ERP

What a generic ERP gets wrong about this industry

Bags are built from components

Body, patch, valve, liner and film each have their own GSM, waste and route. The BOM is component-wise, not one flat line.

Routes vary per construction

A laminated block bottom bag runs seven processes; a plain stitched sack runs four. Routes are data, not customisation projects.

Waste is real money

Kilograms carry the waste of every stage they pass through. "Lost to waste" is money gone, never a figure quietly credited back.

Prices move weekly

Resin rates change; approved orders must keep the rate they were costed at. Versioned prices are the default, not an add-on.

Masters drive discipline

ISO documentation, customer and third-party audits demand structured master data — built in from experience, with an SAP touch.

Works on the shop-floor PC

Offline-first where it matters, with sync where you want it. No "internet is down, production stops".
