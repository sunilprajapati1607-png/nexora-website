16 Years, Four Roles, One Obsession With Fixing the Process

Sunil's career didn't start in software. Four years as a department head, then four years in production planning — where ISO documentation, customer audits and third-party audits made master data discipline non-negotiable, not optional. From there he moved into SAP implementation, rolling out ERP on real shop floors, and today leads as SAP Manager. Every one of those roles is stitched into how Nexora is built.

The tools grew alongside the roles — Excel on the production floor, AppSheet to close the gaps ERP couldn't reach fast enough, then custom software once the problem outgrew both. It's also why this software carries an SAP touch: structured master data, revision history and audit-ready documents aren't an afterthought here — they're how Sunil has run production for 16 years.
